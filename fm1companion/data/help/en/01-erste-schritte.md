# Getting started

Welcome to the **FM-1 Companion Suite**. Everything around your M-VAVE FM-1 lives in one app – on the left in
the sidebar, sorted into *Device*, *Sound*, *Music*, *Data & Tools* and *Help*. Related tools sit as tabs on one page (e.g. Overview › Status, Backups › Browse & Compare, Firmware+ › Simulator).

Everything to do with firmware – finding, trying out, installing, switching versions, going back to the original – is in
one place: [Firmware+](app://page/discover) under *Data & Tools*. Whether there is an update is already shown on the
[Overview](app://page/overview). If the FM-1 is stuck in update mode, the [Recovery](app://page/recovery) page helps
(also under *Data & Tools*).

## 1. Connect the FM-1

1. Switch the FM-1 on and plug it into the computer with a **USB data cable** (charge-only cables do not work).
2. The device appears at the top of the toolbar, e.g. *FM-1 A · Melodee*. The app detects the firmware by itself.
3. Give the device a name: [Overview](app://page/overview) › *Rename*. With two FM-1s, each one gets
   its own colour, its own backups and its own notes.

## 2. Back up first

Before you change anything: **Ctrl+B** backs up the complete device (projects, presets, settings).
More about this under [Backups](help:04-backups).

## 3. Find things quickly

* **Ctrl+K** opens the command search: type “backup”, “simulator” or “midi” and press Enter.
* **F1** opens this help, **F9** hides the sidebar, **F11** switches to full screen.
* **Ctrl+Plus/Minus** enlarges or shrinks the whole user interface.

## 4. Try it without a device

Start the app with `fm1-companion --demo`. You then get three simulated FM-1s – Melodee, SLOOP and one with
a firmware without editor protocol (MIDI only) – with which you can try everything safely.

## 5. Bottom right: your computer

The status bar shows CPU, memory, temperature and free space at a glance. A click opens the
[System check](app://action/syscheck) – it finds, for example, loose USB contacts or a power supply that is too weak.
