# Samples & Kits

On the [Samples & Kits](app://page/samples) page you build kits for the FM-1 from your sounds.

1. **Add folder:** e.g. your Pocket Operator samples. The app scans WAV, AIFF, FLAC and MP3
   (MP3/FLAC need `ffmpeg`).
2. **Listen, select, add labels**, mark favourites.
3. **Into the kit:** up to 16 sounds; the app spreads them over the keys from C4 upwards, trims and normalizes on request.
4. **Upload:** load the kit into a sample slot – this works with firmwares that have sample slots
   (Skiff, SLOOP). Melodee from 1.5 on no longer has sample slots; kits can still be built and saved.

Recording your own samples: [Recording](app://page/recorder) records the USB audio of the FM-1 and opens the
file in Audacity on request.
