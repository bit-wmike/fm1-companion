# Simulator

The simulator runs FM-1 firmwares directly on the computer – handy for trying out a firmware
before you install it, or for building sounds without the device. Open it via [Firmware+ › Simulator](app://page/simulator) or with
**Ctrl+K** › “Simulator”.

* You play with the mouse, the computer keyboard or a MIDI keyboard.
* The sound is a reproduction; timing and load can differ from the real device.
* On the Raspberry Pi 5 the simulators run more or less smoothly depending on the firmware. If there are dropouts,
  close other programs and keep an eye on the temperature in the status bar.
