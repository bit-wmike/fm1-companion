## Community tools

Collected at <https://thefirmwarehub.com/tools/>. **Not checked by us** – use at your own risk,
always make a backup first.

* **M-Flasher** – writes firmware files to the FM-1 (not checked by us).
* **MvaveFM1Unbricker** – brings an FM-1 back from the ROM boot mode “WL82 UBOOT1.00”. An unmodified
  copy is built into the app and runs via [Recovery › Stage 2](app://page/recovery) – with the
  safety rules of the original (double backup, never write below 0x4000). We have not yet tried this path on a real,
  stuck FM-1.
* **FM-1 Transporter** – reads and writes the flash via an RP2040 on the USB data lines; recovery when
  nothing boots any more (not checked by us).
* **Rescue (The Firmware Hub)** – the hub's recovery path for stuck devices (not checked by us).
* **FM1 Editor & Librarian** – edit and manage sounds: <https://fm1-editor.com> (not checked by us).
* **Dexed** – free DX7 software synth, good for preparing FM sounds (not checked by us).
* **fm1-emu** – CPU simulator (calls itself an “emulator”) for starting FM-1 firmwares on the computer (not checked by us).
* **FM1 Workbench (official)** – tool from M-VAVE around the open firmware (not checked by us).
* **FM-1 Workbench (community)** – workbench tool from the community (not checked by us).
* **fm1buddy** – small helper tool for the FM-1 (not checked by us).
